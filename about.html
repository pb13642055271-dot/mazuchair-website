Who We Are

Crafting Quality Seating Since 2010

MAZU was founded in 2010 in Hebei Province, China — one of the country's largest manufacturing hubs. What started as a small workshop producing folding chairs for local markets has grown into a full-scale furniture manufacturer with over 500 employees and an annual output exceeding 2 million units.

Today we serve distributors, traders, procurement agencies, and enterprise clients across Europe, the Middle East, and the Americas. Our commitment remains unchanged: deliver exceptional quality at factory-direct prices, backed by reliable delivery and responsive service.
